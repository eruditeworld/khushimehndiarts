About the Artist

The Art Behind Khushi Mehndi Arts

Khushi Mehndi Arts brings together traditional mehndi artistry and contemporary design to create beautiful patterns for brides, families and special occasions. Every design is discussed around the mood, occasion and personal style of the client, so the final look feels personal rather than off-the-shelf.

  • Detailed artwork with close attention to fine patterns and decorative elements.
  • A blend of traditional Indian inspiration and contemporary bridal styling.
  • Based in Khuldabad, Prayagraj — serving brides and families across the city.
